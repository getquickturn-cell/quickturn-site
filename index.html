Sound familiar?

We've talked with mobile mechanics across Utah. Fixing the truck isn't the hard part. Keeping track of it is.

Invoices at the kitchen table

Road calls end at 9 p.m. Then the notes from the truck, the texts, and the receipts all get typed in again before bed.

Hours you never billed

Drive time, diagnostic time, the extra half hour on a seized bolt. When it isn't written down, it doesn't make the invoice.

Fleets want it done their way

Unit numbers, PO numbers, mileage. Fleet customers pay slower when an invoice is missing something they need.

How it works

Three steps, done from your phone before you leave the yard.

  1. Say it

    Finish the job and talk through it: customer, unit number, parts, hours, PO. Like leaving yourself a voice memo.

  2. Check it

    QuickTurn turns it into an invoice with the customer and unit already filled in. You look it over and fix anything before it goes out.

  3. Send it

    Text or email the invoice on the spot. The job and the truck's history are saved, so next time you know what you did on unit 214.
