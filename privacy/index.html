Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

QuickTurn LLC · Last updated November 17, 2026

Who we are

QuickTurn LLC ("QuickTurn," "we," "us") provides invoicing software and services for mobile mechanics. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account and business details you give us, such as your name, business name, email, mobile number, and billing rates.
  • Job information you send us, including text messages, photos of receipts, voice memos, and customer and vehicle details needed to prepare invoices.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and usage logs, used to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

We use your information to provide the service: preparing, storing, and sending invoices; responding to your messages; supporting your account; and improving QuickTurn. We use trusted service providers (for hosting, text messaging, transcription, and AI processing) only to operate the service on our behalf.

Text messaging (SMS) program

Program: QuickTurn Job Texting. Mechanics who sign up for QuickTurn can text job notes, receipt photos, and voice memos to our number. We reply with confirmations, questions about the job, and invoice-ready notices.

Consent: You opt in by giving us your mobile number when you sign up and texting our number. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase.

Frequency: Message frequency varies based on the jobs you send. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out: Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help, or email getquickturn@gmail.com.

No sharing of mobile information: We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver messages.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that operate QuickTurn for us, when you ask us to (for example, sending an invoice to your customer), or when required by law.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your choices

You may access, correct, or delete your information by contacting us. You can stop texts at any time by replying STOP.

Terms of service

By using QuickTurn you agree to provide accurate job information and to review invoices before sending them to your customers. You are responsible for the final amounts you bill. QuickTurn is provided "as is," and we may update the service or these terms; we'll post changes on this page.
